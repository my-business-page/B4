# B4 — Mum's Ngoh Hiang

## Project status
Blueprint/backbone phase only.

## Business intelligence
- Business: Mum's Ngoh Hiang
- Type: Singapore handmade food / frozen food brand with physical café presence and online marketplace sales
- Brand story evidenced by current storefront/social commerce: started from a humble home recipe and grew into a broader food business.
- Official marketplace: https://shopee.sg/mumsngohhiang
- Current products evidenced on marketplace: handmade frozen pork & prawn ngoh hiang, chicken & prawn, pork-only, fried ngoh hiang, fried meatballs, garlic chilli and bundle sets.
- Instagram: @mumsngohhiang
- WhatsApp: official channel was identified in prior lead research; verify exact number/link before implementation.
- Website status: no standalone business domain identified in lead research.
- Current evidence: active Shopee store with current products and thousands of sales; official social-commerce presence.
- Source: https://shopee.sg/mumsngohhiang

## Website objective
Present the brand as a premium family food business rather than just an Instagram/Shopee seller. The site should connect the story, ready-to-eat food, frozen products, bundles and direct WhatsApp conversion.

## Structural backbone
1. Branded loader.
2. Navigation: Home, Our Story, Menu, Frozen Range, Ready-to-Eat, Gallery, FAQ, Order.
3. Home hero: signature ngoh hiang product photography + family-made positioning + Order CTA.
4. Story: origin, handmade process, family/tradition messaging using verified facts only.
5. Menu/product catalogue: frozen range, ready-to-eat items, chilli and bundles; product data architecture should support variants.
6. Product detail pages/modals with ingredients/allergens only if verified.
7. Gallery: food, packaging, café/storefront and behind-the-scenes.
8. Order: WhatsApp + marketplace links; direct-order pathway should be obvious.
9. FAQ: storage, reheating, pickup/delivery and bulk orders only when verified.
10. Contact/visit: current physical location and hours after verification.
11. Footer: business identity, contacts, social links, marketplace, legal.

## Visual blueprint
- Direction: modern heritage food brand; warm, handmade, premium.
- Palette: deep red/brown, cream, sesame/gold accents sampled from authentic brand imagery.
- Typography: warm display serif + clean sans.
- Imagery: close-up handmade texture, packaging, serving shots; never generic stock ngoh hiang.
- Motion: subtle steam/parallax, ingredient transitions, scroll reveals, tactile product hover.
- Mobile: sticky order CTA, swipeable product categories, easy marketplace/WhatsApp access.

## Conversion
Primary: Order/WhatsApp. Secondary: Shop online, View menu, Our story.

## Hard rules
Do not invent ingredient claims, prices, certification, café address or delivery terms. Product catalogue must be data-driven and editable.
